How it works

1. Post a gig or browse talent. Clients describe the job, budget and timeline, or search verified freelancers by skill and location.
2. Hire with escrow. The payment is held safely by SkillStamp while the work is done.
3. Approve and release. When the work is delivered and approved, the freelancer is paid and the client can rate the work.

Why SkillStamp

  • Verified skills. Freelancers submit work samples and proof of ownership before earning a verified badge.
  • Escrow-protected gigs. Money is only released when you are happy with the work.
  • Built for Africa. Find talent in Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa and across the continent, matched by skills and location.
  • Honest reviews. Ratings come from real completed gigs.
